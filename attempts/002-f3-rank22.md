# 002: look for 3x3x3 rank 22 over F3

## Why F3 and not GF(2)
Rank 22 is now ruled out over F2 and over the integers: Tomar, "The rank of 3x3 matrix
multiplication over F2 is 23" ([arXiv:2610.11823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11823), Oct 2026,
preprint, Lean-checked certificates). It builds on the earlier "at least 22" results
([arXiv:2610.01639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01639)). So a GF(2) or {-1,0,1} search for 22 is pointless.

What this means for the rational case (our own reasoning, to be double-checked):
- If a rank-22 scheme over Q had only odd denominators, reducing it mod 2 would give a rank-22 scheme over F2. That is impossible. So **any rank-22 rational scheme must use an even denominator** (such as 1/2) in every equivalent form.
- 1/2 is a perfectly ordinary element of F3 (1/2 = 2). A rational rank-22 scheme whose denominators avoid 3 reduces to a rank-22 scheme over F3.
- The rank over F3 is open in its own right (the paper above lists it as open).

So F3 is the smallest field where a rank-22 scheme is still possible *and* where rational
schemes of the only remaining kind show up. Our tool already supports it (`--ring Z3`), and our
verifier supports `--mod 3`.

## Hypothesis
Flip-graph walks over F3 explore schemes that GF(2) and {-1,0,1} walks cannot reach (they can use
"1/2"). If rank 22 exists over F3 with a reachable flip path from rank 23, a pool search is the
cheapest way to find it. If it doesn't show up, we learn how the F3 rank-23 landscape differs from
F2 (for example, how many distinct schemes, and how quickly 23 is reached), which shapes 003.

## Plan
0. **Check prior work first.** Has anyone reported an F3 search for 3x3x3 rank 22? Look in Perminov
   ([arXiv:2603.02398](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02398)) and the Kauers–Moosbauer papers and repo.
   If someone has done it at scale, cut stage B and go straight to 003.
1. **Stage A: calibrate.** Add the case `333_Z3_23` to `scripts/bench_fg.py` (10 seeds, 120s cap).
   Make sure `import_fg` reads Z3 schemes (coefficient 2 means -1) and that every result passes
   `matmul.verify --mod 3`. Record the time to rank 23 next to the Z2 and ZT numbers in BENCHMARKS.md.
2. **Stage B: search for 22.** Pool mode, starting from the rank-23 F3 schemes from stage A plus
   known rank-23 schemes reduced mod 3:
   `flip_graph -n1 3 -n2 3 -n3 3 --ring Z3 --use-pool --pool-unique-only --target-rank 22 --save-metrics ...`
   Run it under a hard timeout (the tool has none), with 2 processes at most, and check machine load first.
3. **If a rank-22 scheme appears:** verify it mod 3 with our verifier, check it against the
   catalogue, then try `lift --ring Z3` toward Q. (The lift must produce even denominators, by the
   argument above. A lift with odd denominators only would mean our argument or a verifier is wrong.)

## What counts as progress, and what stops the attempt
- Progress: stage A reaches 23 reliably; the pool of distinct rank-23 F3 schemes keeps growing (the
  landscape is large); metrics show runs stalling at 23 in *different* places rather than the same few.
- Stop: the stage B budget runs out with no 22, or the pool saturates (no new unique 23s). Don't add
  more seeds to the same walk. Change the method instead (see Next).

## Result
**Step 0 (prior work), 2026-11-22.** We found no published F3 search for 3x3x3 rank 22. Perminov
(arXiv:2603.02398) mentions 3x3x3 only when citing others' rank-23 work. We read part of that paper
in full and the rest only as a summary, so this check isn't airtight. A caution from the same paper:
on 3x3x6, F3 searches stalled at rank 42, like GF(2) and {-1,0,1}, while the best rational
scheme has rank 40. So F3 is not automatically stronger.

**Stage A (calibration), 2026-11-22.** `python3 -I scripts/bench_fg.py 333_Z3_23`: 10/10 seeds reached
rank 23, median 0.19s, max 0.65s (120s cap, 1 thread each, 2 at a time). All 10 final schemes passed
`matmul.verify` mod 3, and they are not valid over Q as written. Raw lines: `results/benchmarks.jsonl`
(case `333_Z3_23`).

**Stage B (search for 22), 2026-11-22.** `python3 -I scripts/run_002.py 1800`: two variants side by side,
each with 16 runners on 2 threads, killed at 30 minutes. Lines in `results/runs.jsonl` (attempt `002-f3-rank22`).

| Variant | What it does | Best rank | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| pool | descends rank by rank, keeps 2000 structurally different schemes per level | 23 | the rank-23 level stopped at **164** different schemes after its 1000-iteration limit; then 398 iterations at rank 22 found none |
| walk | free walks with plus moves, sandwiching 0.01, reduce 0.01 | 23 | about 7×10⁹ flips in total (16 runners × ~450M); all runners stuck at 23–25 |

- No rank-22 scheme. All 165 rank-23 schemes saved (164 pool + 1 walk) pass `matmul.verify --mod 3`, and they are all different as coefficient lists.
- Rank 26, 25 and 24 pools filled to 2000 quickly. Rank 23 did not: in 1000 iterations only 164 structurally different schemes turned up. Either rank-23 schemes over F3 are relatively few (up to the tool's notion of "structure"), or the walk keeps returning to the same ones. We can't yet tell which.
- Schemes are kept in `scratch/002/` (not in git).

This doesn't show that rank 22 is impossible over F3. It shows that plain flip-graph search at this
budget doesn't reach it.

## What went wrong / surprised us
- The rank-23 pool was much harder to fill than the higher ranks (164 vs 2000). This is the main new observation.
- Pool mode saves schemes into a `3x3x3/` subfolder. Our first runner version missed them; a 45s dry run caught it.
- Load: 2 processes × 2 threads used about 4 cores. The fans spun up, but macOS recorded no thermal warnings.

## What we would do better
- Before spending time at rank 22, measure the rank-23 landscape properly: count schemes up to the real symmetry
  (equivalence under basis changes and transposition), not the tool's "structure", and check whether new ones
  keep appearing with more time or whether the count levels off.
- Set `--pool-max-iterations` deliberately; the default (1000) decided when the rank-22 phase began.

## Next
If there's no 22, the candidates for 003 are:
- restrict the search to schemes with a symmetry (such as the cyclic symmetry that permutes the
  three factors), which makes the F3 space small enough to exhaust;
- add necessary conditions that a rank-22 scheme would have to satisfy;
- try the ⟨3,3,p⟩ family over F3.
Backup goal: secondary records at rank 23 (fewer additions), though that area is now crowded
(52 additions reported, [arXiv:2609.06588](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.06588)).
